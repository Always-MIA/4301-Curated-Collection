# Mental Health & Well-Being in Aviation

## Project Overview

This project is a curated collection focused on mental health and well-being in the aviation industry, with a primary focus on flight attendants. The collection brings together research, government resources, aviation organizations, and other credible sources that explore the occupational conditions that can affect flight attendants' mental and physical well-being.

As a flight attendant for PSA Airlines with two years of experience, I have firsthand experience with many of the working conditions discussed throughout this collection. The goal of this project is to connect that personal perspective with credible research and resources that can help explain the challenges flight attendants may experience.

## Purpose

The purpose of this collection is to make information about mental health in aviation easier to find and understand. Instead of requiring readers to search through scattered research and websites, the collection organizes relevant sources into categories based on the topics they address.

The collection focuses on:

- Occupational stress and the work environment
- Burnout, fatigue, and sleep
- Psychological health and trauma
- Support and well-being

Each source includes an annotation explaining what the source covers, why it is relevant, who may find it useful, and any limitations or considerations to keep in mind.

## Organization

The collection is organized into four main categories:

### Occupational Stress & Work Environment

Sources covering workplace stress, working conditions, psychosocial factors, workplace relationships, and work-life conflict.

### Burnout, Fatigue & Sleep

Sources examining burnout, fatigue, insomnia, disrupted sleep schedules, and the effects these conditions can have on flight attendants.

### Psychological Health & Trauma

Sources focused on psychological distress, mental health, PTSD, trauma, and the effects of critical incidents.

### Support & Well-Being

Resources addressing mental-health support, peer support, employee assistance programs, critical-incident response, and general well-being within aviation.

Tags are also used to help connect sources that address similar topics across different categories.

## Sources

The collection includes a variety of source types, including:

- Peer-reviewed academic research
- Government resources
- Aviation organizations
- Professional resources
- Educational materials
- Videos

Using different types of sources helps provide multiple perspectives on mental health and well-being in aviation rather than relying on one type of information.

## Intended Audience

This collection is primarily intended for:

- Flight attendants
- Aviation employees
- Aviation students and professionals
- Researchers studying occupational health
- Anyone interested in mental health within the aviation industry

## Project Context

This collection was created as part of a curated information collection assignment. The project demonstrates research, source evaluation, organization, annotation, and digital publishing.

The final collection is published online using Quartz so that the information can be accessed through a web browser without requiring users to download a file.
