# Mental Health & Well-Being in Aviation
### Understanding the people behind the uniform

Working in aviation comes with experiences that are difficult to understand from the outside. Flight attendants manage irregular schedules, long workdays, changing time zones, demanding passengers, time away from home, workplace relationships, and situations that can be physically and emotionally stressful. While these challenges are often treated as simply part of the job, they can have a real effect on a flight attendant's mental health and overall quality of life.

This curated collection explores the mental health and well-being of people working in the aviation industry, with a particular focus on flight attendants. It brings together research, government resources, aviation organizations, educational materials, and other resources to better understand the occupational factors that can contribute to stress, burnout, fatigue, psychological distress, and trauma, as well as the resources available to help aviation professionals cope with these challenges.

### Why I Chose This Topic

I chose this topic because I am not approaching aviation mental health solely as a student or researcher. I have worked as a flight attendant for PSA Airlines for the past two years, and many of the issues discussed throughout this collection are things I have experienced firsthand.

My own experiences have shown me how factors such as scheduling, fatigue, workplace relationships, passenger interactions, base location, and the overall work environment can affect quality of life. These experiences are what led me to look more closely at the research and resources surrounding mental health in aviation and to create a collection that could be useful to other flight attendants and aviation professionals.

### Explore the Collection

The collection is organized into four main areas:

[**Occupational Stress & Work Environment**](stress)
Explores workplace stressors such as workload, scheduling, passenger interactions, workplace relationships, and work-life balance.

[**Burnout, Fatigue & Sleep**](burnout)  
Focuses on burnout, irregular schedules, fatigue, insomnia, sleep disruption, and the effects these factors can have on work and personal life.

[**Psychological Health & Trauma**](trauma)
Examines psychological distress, mental health concerns, traumatic events, and the emotional effects of working in aviation.

[**Support & Well-Being**](support)
Highlights peer support, employee assistance programs, critical incident response, coping strategies, and other resources designed to support aviation professionals.


>**This collection is intended as an informational resource and is not a substitute for professional mental-health care.**
